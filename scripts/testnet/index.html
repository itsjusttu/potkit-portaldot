PotKit v2 testnet helper

Local test tool for the v2 run on Portaldot V3 testnet. It covers what the app has no button for: TinyReceiver, a settle with the wrong amount, redeem from another wallet, withdraw as a contract. Every transaction is signed in your wallet. Simulate is a free eth_call; Send anyway records a transaction that is expected to fail (gas limit 5·10¹³, at most about 0.5 tPOT, only the used gas is charged).

0 · Deploy PotKit v2.1, testnet build (10-minute grace period)

The app's Deploy button deploys the mainnet build (30-day grace period). This deploys the testnet build from npm run compile, which differs from it only in the 4 bytes of the grace constant, so both sides of the grace period can be tested within minutes. Estimated about 365 tPOT (new code: the full storage deposit). After it confirms, the PotKit field is filled and the log has a link that opens the app on this kit.

1 · Deploy TinyReceiver

73-byte Yul contract (scripts/testnet/TinyReceiver.yul). It refuses every plain tPOT transfer, so a PotKit payout to it fails and is credited; only its deployer can make it call PotKit (used for withdraw). Estimated about 22 tPOT, mostly storage deposit.

2 · Settle with a wrong amount

The app's pay drawer always sends exactly the face value, so this sends face − 1 wei or face + 1 wei. Expected: underpaid / overpaid.

3 · Redeem as the connected wallet

Connect wallet B for this. Expected for anyone but the depositor: not depositor. For the depositor after settlement while shares are still unclaimed: claim first.

4 · TinyReceiver: withdraw its credit

Calls PotKit.withdraw(to) as the TinyReceiver (only its deployer can trigger this). Expected after a credited payout: the credit goes to to and the receiver's credit is 0.

5 · Withdraw the connected wallet's own credit

For address C if its payout was credited (C then needs a little tPOT for gas first).

6 · Close a listing as the connected wallet

After settlement anyone may close a listing; the escrowed shares are paid out to the seller, not the caller. Before settlement only the seller can cancel (expected for anyone else: not seller).
